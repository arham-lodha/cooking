Mediterranean Quinoa & Roasted Vegetable Bowl

  • Cuisine: Mediterranean
  • Diet: lacto-vegetarian (no eggs, meat, or fish)
  • Serves: 2
  • Time: prep 10 min / cook 15 min
  • Source: original
  • Added: 2026-09-27

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup quinoa, rinsed
  • 1 cup water or vegetable broth
  • 1 cup roasted vegetables (thawed and warmed)
  • 1 can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 1/4 cup crumbled feta
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley or mint

Vinaigrette

  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 small garlic clove, minced
  • Pinch dried oregano
  • Salt and pepper

Instructions

1. Cook the quinoa at a 1:2 ratio with water or broth — bring to a boil, then cover and simmer 15 minutes until the liquid is absorbed. Fluff and let cool. (Prep-ahead) 2. Whisk the vinaigrette: combine the lemon juice, garlic, oregano, and salt first, then stream in the olive oil slowly while whisking so it emulsifies instead of separating. (Prep-ahead) 3. Day-of: warm the roasted vegetables, then combine with the quinoa and chickpeas. Dress and toss, top with feta and herbs.

Notes / Variations

  • The order matters for the vinaigrette — whisking the acid and aromatics together before adding oil is what keeps it emulsified instead of splitting into oily puddles.
