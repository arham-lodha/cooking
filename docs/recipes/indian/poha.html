Poha (Turmeric-Free)

  • Cuisine: Indian
  • Diet: lacto-vegetarian (no eggs, meat, or fish)
  • Serves: 2
  • Time: prep 5 min / cook 15 min
  • Source: original
  • Added: 2026-09-27

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups poha (flattened rice)
  • 1 tbsp ghee
  • 1/2 tsp mustard seeds
  • Curry leaves, if available (optional)
  • Handful peanuts (optional)
  • 1/2 onion, diced
  • 1 Thai chili, chopped
  • 1/2 tsp Kashmiri chili powder or paprika (for warmth/color, standing in for turmeric)
  • Salt
  • Juice of 1/2 lemon
  • Cilantro, to finish

Instructions

1. Rinse the poha briefly in a sieve under running water just until it softens, about 10-20 seconds — don't soak it, or it turns mushy. Drain and set aside. 2. Heat the ghee, splutter the mustard seeds, then add the curry leaves and peanuts if using, followed by the onion and chili. Sauté until the onion softens. 3. Add the drained poha, chili powder, and salt. Toss gently to combine and warm through, 2-3 minutes. 4. Off heat, finish with lemon juice and cilantro.

Notes / Variations

  • This batch skips turmeric (normally what gives poha its yellow color) since we're out until early October 2026 — a little extra chili powder keeps it flavorful, it'll just look pale rather than yellow this time.
