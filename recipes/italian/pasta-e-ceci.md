# Pasta e Ceci (Pasta & Chickpea Stew)

- **Cuisine:** Italian
- **Diet:** lacto-vegetarian (no eggs, meat, or fish)
- **Serves:** 2
- **Time:** prep 10 min / cook 25 min
- **Source:** original
- **Added:** 2026-09-27

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp olive oil, plus more to finish
- 1/2 onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 can chickpeas, drained (liquid reserved)
- 1.5 cups vegetable broth or water
- 1 sprig rosemary (or 1/2 tsp dried)
- 4 oz small pasta (ditalini or small shells)
- Grated vegetarian parmesan (rennet-free)
- Black pepper

## Instructions

1. **Build the soffritto**: sauté the onion in olive oil over medium heat until soft and translucent, then add the garlic for 30 seconds. (Prep-ahead)
2. Stir in the tomato paste and cook 1-2 minutes until it darkens slightly — this deepens the flavor instead of leaving it raw and sharp.
3. Add the chickpeas, rosemary, and broth. Simmer 15 minutes, then mash about a third of the chickpeas against the side of the pot to thicken the stew. Store as the base. (Prep-ahead)
4. **Day-of:** bring the base back to a simmer, add the pasta directly into it, and cook until al dente — add water or the reserved chickpea liquid if it thickens too much before the pasta is done.
5. Finish off heat with a drizzle of good olive oil, cracked pepper, and grated parmesan.

## Notes / Variations

- Cooking the pasta directly in the stew (rather than separately) lets the starch thicken the dish naturally, similar to a risotto.
- Mashing some of the chickpeas gives body without needing cream.
