# Thai Green Curry with Tofu & Vegetables

- **Cuisine:** Thai
- **Diet:** lacto-vegetarian (no eggs, meat, or fish)
- **Serves:** 2
- **Time:** prep 25 min / cook 20 min
- **Source:** original
- **Added:** 2026-09-27

## Ingredients

**Curry paste**
- 4-5 Thai chilis
- 3 cloves garlic
- 1-inch piece ginger (galangal is more traditional if you can find it)
- 1 stalk lemongrass, white part only, minced (or 1 tbsp lemongrass paste)
- 1/4 onion or 1 small shallot
- Zest of 1 lime
- 1/2 tsp ground coriander
- 1/2 tsp cumin
- Small handful cilantro stems
- 1 tsp soy sauce (stands in for fish sauce)
- Pinch salt

**Curry**
- 1 tbsp neutral oil
- 1 can (13.5 oz) full-fat coconut milk
- 1 block Kirkland tofu, pressed and cubed
- 1 cup mixed vegetables (bell pepper + green beans work well), chopped
- 1 tsp brown sugar
- Extra soy sauce, to taste
- Handful Thai basil leaves
- Lime wedges and cooked jasmine rice, to serve

## Instructions

1. **Press the tofu** for at least 15 minutes, then cube it and pan-sear in a little oil until golden on 2-3 sides. Set aside. (Prep-ahead)
2. **Blend or pound the curry paste** ingredients into a rough paste — a mortar and pestle gives better texture than a blender, but either works. Pound the fibrous stuff (lemongrass, ginger) first before adding softer aromatics. (Prep-ahead)
3. **Day-of:** heat the oil in a pot, fry the curry paste for 2-3 minutes until fragrant and it starts to split slightly from the oil — that visual cue means the paste is properly cooked.
4. Add the coconut milk and bring to a gentle simmer. Don't let it boil hard, or the coconut fat can split.
5. Add the vegetables, simmer 5 minutes, then add the seared tofu and simmer 3 more minutes.
6. Season with brown sugar and soy sauce to taste. Off heat, stir in the Thai basil.
7. Serve over rice with a lime wedge.

## Notes / Variations

- No turmeric in this batch — the green color comes from the chilis and herbs, so it isn't missed.
- Fish sauce is swapped for soy sauce to keep this vegetarian; a vegetarian "fish sauce" (fermented soy-based) works too if you have it.
