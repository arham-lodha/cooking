# Couscous & Chickpea Salad

- **Cuisine:** Mediterranean
- **Diet:** lacto-vegetarian (no eggs, meat, or fish)
- **Serves:** 2
- **Time:** prep 10 min / cook 5 min
- **Source:** original
- **Added:** 2026-09-27

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup couscous
- 1/2 cup boiling water or broth
- Remaining roasted vegetables and chickpeas (last of the can)
- 1/4 cucumber, diced
- Handful cherry tomatoes, halved
- 2 tbsp crumbled feta

**Dressing**
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- Pinch dried oregano
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Pour the boiling water or broth over the couscous, cover, and let it steam for 5 minutes. Then fluff with a fork — don't stir while it's hot, or it turns gummy. (Day-of or morning-ahead)
2. Toss the couscous with the remaining roasted vegetables, chickpeas, cucumber, and tomato.
3. Dress and top with feta. Serve at room temperature or chilled.

## Notes / Variations

- Fluffing with a fork instead of stirring is what keeps couscous light and separate rather than clumping into a paste.
- Good use for the last of the week's chickpeas and roasted vegetables.
