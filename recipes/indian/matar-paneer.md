# Matar Paneer (Turmeric-Free)

- **Cuisine:** Indian
- **Diet:** lacto-vegetarian (no eggs, meat, or fish)
- **Serves:** 2
- **Time:** prep 15 min / cook 30 min
- **Source:** original
- **Added:** 2026-09-27

## Ingredients

- 0.83 lb paneer, cubed
- 1 tbsp ghee, plus more for searing
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic + 1-inch ginger, made into a paste
- 1-2 Thai chilis, chopped (standing in for green chili)
- 2 tomatoes, or 1 cup crushed canned tomato
- 1/2 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- 1/2 tsp Kashmiri chili powder (for color, standing in for turmeric)
- 1/2 cup peas (frozen is fine)
- 1/4 tsp garam masala
- Pinch kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves), crushed
- Salt
- Splash cream or milk (optional)
- Cilantro, to finish

## Instructions

1. **Sear the paneer**: pan-sear the cubes in a little ghee until light golden on a couple of sides — don't overdo it, or it turns tough. Set aside. (Prep-ahead)
2. **Brown the onion**: cook the onion in ghee over low-medium heat until deep golden brown, 12-15 minutes. This is the real flavor base of the curry — don't rush it. (Prep-ahead)
3. Add the ginger-garlic-chili paste, cook 1 minute, then add the tomatoes and spices (no turmeric). Cook down until the oil starts to separate at the edges of the masala — that's the sign it's done. Store as the masala base. (Prep-ahead)
4. **Day-of:** reheat the masala base with a splash of water, add the peas, and simmer 4-5 minutes. Add the seared paneer and warm through for 2-3 minutes — don't overcook it or it toughens.
5. Finish with garam masala, the crushed kasuri methi, and an optional splash of cream. Top with cilantro and serve with rice or roti.

## Notes / Variations

- Turmeric mostly adds color, not the core flavor of the curry — a well-browned onion base does most of the real work, so skipping it is barely noticeable here. Kashmiri chili powder adds warm color in its place.
- This batch skips turmeric because we're out until early October 2026.
